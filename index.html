Five steps to carb counting: choosing foods, finding carbs, setting goals with a dietitian, reading the Nutrition Facts table (including subtracting fibre) and tracking how foods affect your blood sugar.

Diabetes Canada

Lows and Highs: Blood Sugar Levels

How to treat a low: 15 g of fast-acting carbohydrate (such as glucose tablets or 150 mL of juice or regular pop), wait 15 minutes, recheck, and repeat until you're above 4 mmol/L.

Diabetes Canada handout, available on diabetes.ca.
